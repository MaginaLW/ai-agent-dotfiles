# Paper Polish

## Purpose

Polish academic drafts without changing the author's meaning.

## Steps

1. Identify unclear claims and improve argument flow and sentence clarity within the requested scope.
2. Preserve equations, symbols, numbers, units, citations, comparison conditions, uncertainty and the strength of each claim. Do not invent evidence or upgrade an observation into a causal or universal conclusion.
3. Check the revision against the source. Flag scientific ambiguities or missing evidence instead of silently resolving them; do not run experiments or alter results as part of polishing.
4. Deliver the requested text and briefly note substantive edits and unresolved issues in the user's language. For a requested Meng-informed CMAME workflow, use `meng-cmame-writing` when available and follow its task-specific evidence rules.

When editing Word, LaTeX or another structured source, preserve its editable equations, citation fields and structure. Verify them with the project's applicable tools; report any aspect that could not be checked.
